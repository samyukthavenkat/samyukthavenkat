# Credit Memo Summary — Volkswagen Financial Services AG

## 1. Summary view

The supplied memo's preliminary credit view, dated 13 October 2026, is **solid with a negative bias**. It describes steady earnings, diversified funding and regulatory-compliant liquidity, balanced against declining CET1, rising BEV residual-value risk and close linkage to Volkswagen AG.

VW FS AG is described as the wholly owned captive finance arm of Volkswagen AG, with €202.3bn of assets and 18.7m contracts at FY2025.

## 2. Financial position

FY2025 figures in the memo include gross revenue of €38,894m, operating result of €2,360m, profit before tax of €2,436m, profit after tax of €1,580m, operating margin on gross revenue of 6.1%, cost/income ratio of 41.0%, PBT return on equity of 7.6%, financial debt/equity of 4.99x and equity ratio of 16.0%.

H2 2024 is a six-month restated comparative. The current group perimeter was formed on 1 July 2024; FY2023 relates to a different predecessor group. The memo cautions against interpreting headline FY2025 growth as a like-for-like annual growth rate.

## 3. Debt-servicing capacity

The memo reports FY2025 interest cover of 1.53x and cash interest cover of 3.16x. It describes portfolio growth as externally funded and identifies customer deposits, the self-liquidating receivables and lease-asset book, parent support and market access as relevant repayment sources.

## 4. Key industry risks

- **EV transition / residual values (High):** guaranteed residual values rose 22% to €64.1bn; first-generation BEV residual values are described as under pressure; FY2025 lease-asset impairments were €675m.
- **Capital intensity (High):** RWA growth outpaced equity growth, and CET1 declined from 18.1% to 16.3%.
- **Competition (Medium):** volumes are linked to Volkswagen Group deliveries; penetration is already 56.7%.
- **Supply chain and tariffs (Medium):** the memo identifies indirect effects on registrations, used-car values and dealer stock financing, without quantifying exposure.
- **Dealer and fleet credit (Medium):** corporate default rate increased from 3.2% to 3.7%; Stage 3 allowances rose approximately 21%.
- **Funding and interest-rate risk (Medium):** wholesale maturities, deposit stability and funding-cost sensitivity remain diligence topics.
- **Parent / rating linkage (High):** the credit view is linked to Volkswagen Group credit quality and the support arrangements described in the memo.

## 5. Further diligence questions

The memo raises questions covering borrower legal entity and parent support, pro-forma results, BEV residual-value allocation, CET1 trajectory, Stage 3 dealer/fleet exposures, backup-line terms and asset encumbrance, prior-year restatements/legal provisions, and downgrade sensitivity.

## 6. Limitations

The memo states that it reflects filings to March 2026 and excludes later results and market data. It is an analyst judgement, not a rating. The analysis should not be represented as an official view of VW FS AG or as a lending recommendation.
